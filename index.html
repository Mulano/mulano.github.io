About

I've spent my career on the unglamorous part of software that decides whether a team ships confidently or nervously: branching models, merge gates, rollbacks, environments, and secrets. I design for the failure case first — blast radius, permissions, and what happens when an automated change is wrong. I'm now applying that same thinking to AI-assisted delivery, where the hard question is which work an agent should own, which needs a human, and where the guardrails go.
